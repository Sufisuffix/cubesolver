Rubik's Cube Helper

Enter your cube, then follow each solving move at your own pace.

Paint the stickers to match your cube. When you submit, the app finds a solution and shows an animated walkthrough with autoplay and a true step-by-step button.

How to enter your cube

  1. Pick one front face and keep the cube orientation consistent.
  2. Fill in the Up, Right, Front, Down, Left, and Back faces.
  3. Use Step by step if you want to advance one move at a time.
